\paragraph{Source selection.}
Generation outcomes come from the 24 result records under the archive's
\texttt{confirmatory\_runs} directory. A first-pass completion is a complete
artifact with no recorded localized generation retry. Retry lists for some
failed cases were not preserved in the original format; we therefore do not
estimate total repair incidence from those lists. Failed cases remain failed.
The 21 execution sources are the corrected
\texttt{runtime\_trajectories\_v2/replicate\_*} records. Earlier runs affected
by human placement and transport defects are not substituted or pooled.
All raw result and trajectory records used here are frozen with SHA-256 hashes
and recomputed by the bundled audit script.

\paragraph{Input scope of the earlier construction comparison.}
The ten requests in Section~\ref{sec:construction} are \emph{world briefs}:
some include several sentences specifying institutions, cast size, room
requirements, and visual style. They do not test strictly one-sentence input.
For the comparative questionnaire, the archived single-pass prompt contains
an original-request JSON exactly matching the corresponding compositional
request for all ten selected first replicates. This confirms a shared user
request; the method-specific system instructions and total computation differ.
The three later prompts are separate, genuinely one-sentence semantic inputs.

\paragraph{Execution schedule and observability.}
Each four-round block is planned from one snapshot; intermediate actions can
make later planned actions invalid. At each checkpoint the policy receives
room connectivity, all-agent summaries, the action catalog, up to four open
action examples, the current intervention, and the 24 most recent events.
Decoding uses the recorded temperature 0.75 and low thinking setting. There is
no archived claim of matched random seeds across providers. A model generates
and controls its own world, so world difficulty, generation quality, and
policy quality are confounded. The harness is an executable-plan stress test,
not an information-asymmetric social experiment.

\paragraph{Scripted human and recovery probes.}
The round-5 request and round-21 follow-up are supplied by the harness.
When needed, the harness moves the human into an occupied room before a
request or rejection probe; this movement is not counted among model actions.
The round-9 probe requests transfer of an item the human does not possess.
All 21 such probes are rejected without a state-hash change. A resource shock
at round 13 and a cross-room instruction at round 17 are likewise scripted.
Target acceptance in model-generated proposals is a declared field, not an
independently elicited human response.

\paragraph{What counts as a consequence.}
The inventory/location endpoint includes successful movement as well as
inventory effects, and therefore is not a measure of economic transformation
alone. The non-speech endpoint includes temporary statuses and describes the
requested effects of approved proposals; approval does not establish that
every intended semantic consequence occurred. The 147 failed model actions
contain both before/after hashes and a false state-change flag. Five references
to nonexistent event IDs occur in failed Gemini 2.5 Flash actions; no successful
action in the archive has such an unknown reference. Thread labels and event
references are supplied by the planner, so retaining them is not independent
proof of causal coherence.

\paragraph{Statistics and reproducibility.}
The independent scope is 24 generation cells and 21 trajectory records, not
1,512 independent experimental units. We report counts and the observed range
over three runs, without asymptotic confidence intervals, significance stars,
or a composite leaderboard. The audit reads raw outcomes and mutations and
does not load the identity-visible expert review or its weighted scores.
The source snapshot also includes the planner harness, coordinator validation,
and custom-action handler used to interpret the records. Source inspection
supports the mechanism explanation but does not replace fresh execution tests
against an immutable historical checkout.
