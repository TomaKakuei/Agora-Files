\end{itemize}

\item Gantry Rigging Strain Relief \& Abyss Purge Loop · Riggers monitor cable tension under heavy debt traffic, dumping abandoned vow canisters into the depths or conducting emergency repairs, directly modifying state\_bridge\_strain and state\_promise\_backlog. · Pulley cables are fraying under physical promise weight, risking a catastrophic bridge snap if cargo is not cut.
\end{itemize}
\heading{人物与目标}\begin{itemize}
\item Tavish Vane · Senior Balance Bailiff — Force the Gantry Runners Syndicate to surrender defaulted promise ampoules to relieve state\_bridge\_strain
\item Maren Kye · Gantry Line Marshal — Re-route courier traffic across secondary spans to prevent bridge failure without paying Excise surcharges
\item Silas Fenn · Silence Mortar Scrivener — Dissolve toxic public vows to create structural mortar, challenging Excise hoarding of broken pledges
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}
\heading{人物与目标}\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\item Chain-Gate Bondhouse — Bridge wardens review household safety bonds and chain crossings for delinquent vowweight. Reduced bridge\_access here cuts merchant trade at the Bridgehead Exchange afterward.
\end{itemize}

\item Seal a promise bundle
\item Tender a safety bond
\end{itemize}
\heading{玩家进入方式}\begin{itemize}

\end{itemize}
\heading{规则与制度}\begin{itemize}
\item All citizens must maintain a minimum weight of 60kg while in public halls.
\item Unlicensed buoyancy exceeding 0.5 meters from the floor is a finable offense.
\item Tampering with a municipal plumb line is considered high treason.
\end{itemize}
\heading{活动与压力}\begin{itemize}
\item The Vector Cycle · Acquire a license, retrieve a rod from the vault, calibrate it in the workshop, and use it to access high-gravity zones. · Rods decay over time and must be recharged or replaced.
\item The Ballast Trade · Buy low-density ceramic, trade it for high-density lead, and use the weight to stabilize yourself in low-g areas or sell for profit. · Market prices fluctuate based on the Archive's current gravity reserves.
\end{itemize}
\heading{人物与目标}\begin{itemize}
\item Archivist Vane · Chief Registrar — To recover the 'Primal Vector' stolen by a buoyancy cult.
\item Merchant Kael · Ballast Broker — To corner the market on high-density ceramic.
\item Arbitrator Nox · Gravity Judge — To expose the corruption in the vector licensing process.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\linewidth}\fontsize{9}{12}\selectfont\raggedright\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
\heading{地点与用途}\begin{itemize}
\item The Intake Atrium — Processing new gravity claimants and weighing visitors.
\item Vector Licensing Vault — Storage of active gravity certificates and vector-rods.
\item The Ballast Exchange — Trading physical weights to offset vector fluctuations.
\end{itemize}
\heading{物件}\begin{itemize}
