This supplement accompanies \emph{Agora: One Sentence, One Living World}.
It contains reproducibility details, complete construction and execution audits,
planner-input and action-space diagnostics, historical records, the returned
visual-survey protocol and counts, internal
procedure controls, a prospective memory-intervention design, and case studies.

the main paper are explicitly identified; bibliography numbers are local to
